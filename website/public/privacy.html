Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

The Short Version

  • We access your email to help you manage it
  • We don't sell your data — ever
  • Your email content is encrypted
  • You can delete everything at any time
  • AI reads your emails, humans don't

1. Introduction

Teeks ("we," "us," or "our") is an AI-powered email assistant. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our Service.

We take your privacy seriously. Because Teeks accesses your email, we want to be transparent about what we do with your data and why.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you sign up, we collect your email address, name, and profile picture from Google OAuth.

2.2 Email Data

When you connect your Gmail account, we access:

  • Email messages — Subject, body, sender, recipients, timestamps
  • Email metadata — Thread IDs, labels, read/unread status
  • Attachments — Metadata only (we do not store attachment contents)

2.3 AI-Processed Data

We generate and store email summaries, extracted tasks, draft replies, and priority classifications.

2.4 Payment Information

Processed by Polar. We do not store credit card numbers or bank details. We receive subscription status and billing history summary only.

3. How We Use Your Information

Purpose Data Used
Provide email triage and summaries Email content, metadata
Extract tasks from emails Email content
Generate reply drafts Email content, context
Process payments Subscription info
Provide customer support Account info, usage data

4. How We Process Your Emails

What we don't do:

  • We don't read your emails manually (only AI processing)
  • We don't sell your email content to anyone
  • We don't use your emails for advertising
  • We don't share email content with third parties except AI processors

We use Google's Gemini API to process your emails. This provider is contractually bound to protect your data and does not retain your data for their own training.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Encryption at rest — Email bodies encrypted using AES-256
  • Encryption in transit — All connections use TLS 1.3
  • Access controls — Role-based access, principle of least privilege
  • Data isolation — Each user's data is logically isolated

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your data
  • Portability — Request your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent — Revoke consent at any time

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@teeks.ai or use in-app settings.

9. Google API Compliance

Teeks' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We only use Google user data to provide the features described in this policy
  • We don't use Google user data for advertising
  • We don't allow humans to read your data unless required for support (with consent), security, or legal compliance

10. Children's Privacy

Teeks is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app notification. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
