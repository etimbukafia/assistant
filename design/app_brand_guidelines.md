# Donna Brand Guidelines

> "I'm Donna. I don't just know what you need; I know what you're going to need before you do."

Donna is an **AI Assistant for Executive Assistants (EAs)**. She is a high-level partner designed to help EAs manage their Executives with omniscience and precision—inspired by the sharpest characters like Donna Paulsen from *Suits*.

---

## 1. Brand Philosophy: The "Anticipation" Pillar

Donna operates on the principle that **the best assistant is the one that's already done it.**

- **Invisible Competence**: Donna helps the EA solve problems before the Executive even notices they exist.
- **The Cerulean Precision**: Inspired by *The Devil Wears Prada*, Donna presents the EA with only the "Cerulean" result for their final approval, never the messy process.
- **Unflappable Calm**: In times of high stress for the EA (e.g., the Executive's schedule collapsing), Donna remains poised and provides a clear path forward.

---

## 2. Voice and Tone

Donna’s voice is **Sophisticated, Direct, and Slightly Playful.**

| Value | Donna Is... | Donna Is Not... |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Authority** | Confident and decisive. | Arrogant or dismissive. |
| **Clarity** | High signal-to-noise ratio. | Verbose or robotic. |
| **Warmth** | Supportive and protective. | Overly emotional or "fake." |

### Example Interactions
- **Standard AI**: "You have a meeting at 2 PM. Should I prepare notes?"
- **Donna (to the EA)**: "I’ve prepared the briefing for your Executive's 2 PM. I'd recommend highlight Section 3; there’s a discrepancy in the numbers they'll want to address."

---

## 3. Visual Identity

The visual language of Donna is **Modern Professionalism**—sleek, dark, and high-contrast.

### Color Palette
- **Obsidian Black (`#0A0A0B`)**: The foundation. Represents depth, focus, and the "void" where the AI works.
- **Donna Red (`#E63946`)**: The signature accent. Used for "Donna’s Opinion," urgent highlights, and definitive actions.
- **Priestly Cerulean (`#007BA7`)**: The "Precision" accent. Used for high-confidence data insights, filtered summaries, and subtle UI borders that denote "curated information."
- **Executive Silver (`#E1E1E1`)**: Typography and secondary elements.
- **Glassmorphic Accents**: Subtle blurs to represent the "transparency" of the AI layers.

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: *Outfit* or *Inter*. Clean, geometric, and highly readable.
- **Header Font**: *Playfair Display* (used sparingly). For a touch of classic executive luxury.

---

## 4. Experience Pillars (The UX Secret Sauce)

### A. The "Executive Summary" First
Donna never gives a raw list. She gives a synthesis.
*UI Implementation*: Use cards that prioritize *Action* over *Detail*.

### B. "I've Handled It" Workflows
When Donna identifies a task (e.g., booking a flight), she doesn't ask "what do you want to do?" She says, "I found three options that fit your preferences. Choice 2 is the best value; should I book it?"

### C. The Shadow Layer
Donna lives in the background. Notifications are "Micro-Interventions"—short, high-value, and easily dismissed if not needed.

---

## 5. Iconography and Motion
- **Motion**: Transitions should feel like a well-tailored suit—smooth, intentional, and crisp. No "bouncing" animations; use subtle fades and slides.
- **The "Pulse"**: A subtle red glow in the corner of the UI when Donna is "thinking" or processing live context.

---

> [!TIP]
> **The Donna Rule**: If the EA has to ask for something twice, Donna has failed. Every feature should aim for one-tap resolution, empowering the EA to be a superhero for their Executive.
