# Teeks Brand Guidelines

> *"Teeks doesn't just help you do your job. It makes your job feel different."*

Teeks is an AI tool built specifically for Executive Assistants. Not for everyone. For them.

The brand is built on one conviction: **an EA's authority is invisible to most people, and nobody has ever built software that honours it.** Teeks does.

---

## 1. Brand Character

Teeks is not a chatbot. It is not an assistant to the assistant. It is a **high-speed drafting partner** — sharp, discreet, always ready, never demanding attention.

| Quality | Teeks is... | Teeks is never... |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sharp** | Precise, decisive, no filler | Verbose, vague, hedging |
| **Discreet** | Quiet interface, no drama | Flashy, noisy, celebratory |
| **Reliable** | Consistent in every state | Surprising, unpredictable |
| **Personal** | Built for EAs specifically | Generic, enterprise-bland |

---

## 2. Visual Identity — The Permanent Ink Palette

The visual language is **Permanent Ink on Quality Paper**. Think a fountain pen on a Smythson notebook. Warm, deliberate, refined.

### Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `bg-base` | `#F5F0E8` | Ivory Paper — the desk surface |
| `bg-surface` | `#FFFFFF` | Card and sheet surfaces |
| `accent-peony` | `#C2185B` | The brand moment — CTA, send, active |
| `accent-brass` | `#A07850` | Action mode — warm secondary |
| `accent-sage` | `#4D7C0F` | Reflection mode — calm secondary |
| `accent-slate` | `#334155` | Precision — links, informational |
| `text-primary` | `#18181B` | Ink Black |
| `text-secondary` | `#71717A` | Stone |
| `urgent` | `#9B1C1C` | Carmine — errors, urgent only |
| `border` | `#E4E0D8` | Hairline |

### The Peony Rule
Peony `#C2185B` appears **once per screen, on the primary action only**. It is the moment the interface says: *this is what matters right now*. Dilute it and it loses its power entirely.

### Typography

- **Playfair Display** — Display and H1/H2 only. The serif brings authority and warmth. Never below 20pt.
- **Inter** — Everything else. Neutral, professional, exceptional at small sizes. No other sans-serif.

## 3. Voice & Tone

Teeks sounds like a sharp colleague who respects your time. Not a software product. Not an enthusiastic chatbot.

**The register is**: calm, informed, confident. One degree warmer than clinical.

### Rules
- No exclamation marks. Anywhere. Ever.
- No passive voice. "I've drafted a reply" not "A reply has been drafted."
- No jargon. "Session" is fine. "AI-powered contextual workflow" is not.
- Short sentences preferred. If a sentence needs a comma to survive, split it.

### Voice Examples

| Situation | ❌ | ✅ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Syncing | "Loading your data, please wait!" | "Syncing your inbox." |
| Empty state | "Nothing here yet! Start a chat to get going!" | "Your conversations appear here." |
| Error | "Oops! Something went wrong." | "That didn't send. Try again." |
| Success | "Great! Your message has been sent successfully!" | "Sent. Thread archived." |
| Action card | "Would you like me to help you with this?" | "Here's a draft. Send or edit." |

---

## 4. Experience Philosophy

### One tap. Every time.
If an EA has to make more than one decision to resolve something, the product has failed. Every card, every action, every suggestion should lead to a single tap resolution.

### The tool disappears.
When Teeks is working well, the EA stops noticing it. They just feel faster. That is the goal.

### Prepared, not prompted.
Teeks doesn't ask "what would you like to do?" It says "here is what's ready for your approval." The preparation already happened. The EA just confirms.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Brand Test**: Show the interface to someone who doesn't know what Teeks is. Before you explain anything, ask them: "Who is this for?" If the answer is not immediately "someone who works at a very high level," the design has work to do.
